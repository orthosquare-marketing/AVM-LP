Got Doubts ? We've Got All Answers

Are Clear Aligners painful?

No. The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so you feel minimal discomfort. Most patients resume normal activities within a day.

How long does aligner treatment take?

Most aligner treatments take 6 to 18 months depending on the complexity of your case. You'll receive an exact timeline after your free 3D scan.

Can I eat and drink normally with aligners?

Yes. Simply remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water, then place them back in after brushing.

Are clear aligners noticeable?

Clear aligners are virtually invisible. Made from transparent medical-grade material, most people won't notice you're wearing them.

Do I need to wear aligners all day?

For best results, wear them 20–22 hours a day, removing only for meals, brushing, and flossing.

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental Implants are titanium posts placed into the jawbone to replace missing teeth. These implants serve as a foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures, restoring natural tooth function and appearance. They offer a long-lasting solution that prevents bone loss, supports jaw structure, and provides a stable, comfortable alternative to traditional dentures or bridges.

Got Doubts ? We’ve Got All Answers

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a titanium post placed into the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root, topped with a natural-looking crown, bridge, or denture.

Who needs dental implants?

Anyone with one or more missing teeth, loose dentures, or damaged teeth that need extraction can be a candidate. A consultation and clinical evaluation confirms suitability.

How long does the procedure take?

Most dental implant procedures are planned after a consultation and clinical evaluation. Your dentist will explain the treatment timeline, comfort options, recovery guidance, and cost estimate based on your case.

Is the procedure painful?

No. The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so you feel minimal discomfort. Most patients resume normal activities within a day.

What’s the cost of implants?

Dental implants at AVM Smiles start at just ₹10,999. Your final estimate depends on the number of implants and your clinical needs - book a free consultation for a personalized quote.

Are implants better than dentures?

Implants are fixed, stable, and feel like natural teeth - no slipping or removal. They also preserve jawbone density, unlike traditional dentures.
